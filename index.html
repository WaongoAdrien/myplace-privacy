MyPlace Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

MyPlace (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is a rental management app for landlords and tenants, published by Faso Studio 101. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

When you use MyPlace we may collect and store the following:

  • Account information: your email address, password (handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see it in readable form), display name, optional phone number, and your account role (tenant or landlord).
  • Rental records you or your landlord enter: properties, units, tenants, leases, rent amounts and due dates, payment records, expenses, and maintenance requests.
  • Messages you send to other users of the app.
  • Files you choose to upload, such as photos, PDFs and other documents (for example lease documents or maintenance photos). Files are uploaded only when you select them.
  • Notification token: if you turn on push notifications, a device token is stored so we can send you notifications.
  • Preferences on your device: your theme choice (light or dark) and whether you dismissed the notification prompt, saved locally on your device.

MyPlace does not collect your precise location, contacts, or camera or microphone data in the background. The app displays no advertising and does not include third-party advertising or analytics tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and sign you in
  • To show you the properties, leases, payments, documents and requests that belong to you
  • To let landlords and tenants communicate and track maintenance requests
  • To send notifications you have opted into
  • To keep the app secure and working properly

3. Payments

MyPlace does not process payments itself. It can show payment details (such as a Venmo or Zelle handle) and open the payment app or copy the details when you tap them. The payment itself happens in that third-party service under its own terms and privacy policy. MyPlace stores only the payment records that you or your landlord enter.

4. Who Can See Your Information

  • Other users in your rental: a landlord can see the tenant, lease, payment and request information for their own properties, and a tenant can see their own lease, payments and messages. Information is shared between the people connected through the same rental so the app can work.
  • Service providers: we use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage and Cloud Messaging) to run the app. Your data is stored and processed on Google's infrastructure under Google's terms and privacy policy.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

5. Data Storage and Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with Firebase, which applies industry-standard security. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your password private.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from within the app: open Settings and choose Delete Account. You can also request deletion by emailing us at the address below. When an account is deleted we remove your personal data from our systems within a reasonable time, except where we must keep information to meet legal obligations or where records are shared with another party in the same rental (such as a lease record held by your landlord).

7. Your Choices

  • Turn push notifications on or off in the app or in your device settings.
  • Update your account information in the app.
  • Request a copy or correction of your data by contacting us.
  • Delete your account as described above.

8. Children's Privacy

MyPlace is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
